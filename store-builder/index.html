FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know coding or design?

No. You answer a few questions, add your products and pick your colours. The themes are designed to look professional with whatever you add.

How do I receive orders and payments?

When a customer checks out, they send the complete order to your WhatsApp in one tap. They can pay the exact amount to your UPI ID from any UPI app, or choose cash on delivery. Money goes straight to your bank account.

How does the mobile app work?

Your store is a Progressive Web App. Customers on Android see an “Install” button; on iPhone they use Share → Add to Home Screen. It gets your icon and opens full screen. To list it on Google Play or the App Store, the free PWABuilder tool can package it. Google charges a one-time developer fee and Apple an annual one.

Do I need to set up hosting or a website?

No. We host your store for you. It goes live at its own link as soon as you finish setup, and any change you make is published automatically. There are no servers, files or renewals to think about.

Where is my data stored?

Your store and orders are stored securely on our servers, so you can manage them from any device once you connect your Google account. Your customers' details are visible only to you, and you can download a full backup any time.
