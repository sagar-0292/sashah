About SnapList by Sahaay

SnapList, made by Sahaay, turns a product photo into a ready-to-publish listing — a title, a description, bullet highlights and tags — written by AI, in the time it takes to make a cup of tea.

Why it exists

Writing a good product listing takes longer than most sellers expect. You need a title that gets found in search, a description that actually persuades someone to buy, and the right keywords — for every single item you list, over and over. For a small seller managing a growing catalogue on Amazon, Etsy, Instagram or their own store, that adds up to real hours each week that could go toward sourcing, packing, or just running the business.

SnapList exists to take that one task off your plate: point it at a photo, and it does the writing.

How it works, briefly

Open it and try it — no signup, no API key needed, right away. For unlimited and fully private use, you can optionally bring your own free API key from an AI provider (Google Gemini or OpenRouter) and paste it into Settings once; from then on, your photos and details go directly from your phone or browser to that AI provider, never through a server we run. See the full How it works page for the step-by-step version.

Who it's for

  • Independent sellers on Amazon, Etsy, Flipkart, Shopify or eBay
  • Instagram/WhatsApp catalogue sellers who need quick, consistent product copy
  • Anyone who'd rather spend their time on the product than on the paragraph describing it

Our approach to your data

If you add your own API key, nothing about your photos or generated listings passes through a server we operate — the calls go directly from your device to the AI provider you chose. If you use the default free option instead, your photo passes through our server just long enough to forward it to our AI provider and return the result; we don't store it. Full details are on the Privacy Policy page.
